ABOUT THE PROJECT

A simple conversion-focused funnel concept for a fitness coaching brand.

SummitFit Coaching is a sample funnel project created to demonstrate my approach to landing page design and lead generation.

The concept focuses on a clear fitness offer, prominent calls to action, and a simple path that guides visitors toward requesting a free plan.

Unlike my larger client case studies, this project is intentionally concise and primarily showcases the visual structure and funnel layout.
