ABOUT THE WORK

Project-based creative support across foundation programs.

The Taylor LEAD Foundation is the larger foundation connected to my client Angela Taylor. My involvement here is lighter and more project-specific than my work with The Dignitas Agency.

I support selected initiatives when graphics, program materials, recruitment assets, scholarship collateral, or campaign content are needed. The work below is organized into two areas: Foundation & Staff Programs, and Game On! Sports 4 Girls.

This page is intentionally more focused than the Dignitas case study: it highlights the specific creative outputs I contributed instead of presenting the Foundation as a full-time client engagement.
